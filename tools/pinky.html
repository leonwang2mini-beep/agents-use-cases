Pinky New

Apache-2.0 desktop app (macOS, Windows, Linux) that lets Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any MCP agent operate a real iPhone or Android phone while you watch, with a gateway that blocks finance apps and holds payments or other risky taps until you approve, plus a replay of every step
